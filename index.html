    How to run the program

    Speed Mode

    For each drill, start with a comfortable par. This means something you can easily complete without difficulty. Upon 3 consecutive Successful Reps, lower the par time by .1 or .2. Successful Reps are defined as all of the following: Beat Par, Good Grip, Good Reload (if applicable), and Called Shots. Note that I specifically do not care about the accuracy on target of your reps, only that you saw what you did. You will fail. Not only is that intended, it is critical.

    Match Mode

    There are no par times for Match Mode. Each drill is to be executed by putting the sights on the target and calling acceptable shots. Repeatability with minimal error is the goal. Make up misses and uncalled shots

    Warm Up

    Set a timer for 5-10 minutes. Engage targets at will. Stretch your fingers. Shake your hands out. Get them warm and ready to work. This is also a good opportunity to play with drills that you find particularly difficult to execute subconsiously.

    Daily Drills

    Set a timer for at least 5 minutes and follow the instructions outlined above. Your goal is to get to the limit of your ability to see and manipulate the gun. This is the stimulus required to elicit the adaptation of increased speed.

    Erase every par, rep, note, completed drill and day, and the activity log?
